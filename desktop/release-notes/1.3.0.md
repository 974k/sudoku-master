- **Sudoku AI**: endless new puzzles made on your device, at the difficulty you choose or with the Randomizer.
- Puzzles now unlock one at a time, and completing a stage opens the next one.
- The app now tells you when an update is available and what's new in it. You decide when to install, and every update is signature-checked.
- Press F11 for full screen. The board and menus are arranged to make good use of the whole screen.
- Optional window transparency in Settings → Appearance.
- Fixed: leaving full screen now returns a maximized or snapped window to exactly where it was.
